# GTA VI Twitch CCU forecast

The model uses the print for UTC hour 1 as the launch's scale, then applies empirical log-ratio quantiles from the kit's console midnight-local comparables. In LOO over comparable launch days, this subset scored 0.3304 mean pinball loss versus 0.3568 for persistence on the same 276 scored hour/comparable pairs. Using every launch type scored 0.7139, so simultaneous-PC and early-access series are excluded. The comparison is small and heterogeneous; it is evidence for a conservative comparable filter, not a guarantee for GTA VI.

Across the comparables, the median viewer ratio to hour 1 is about 0.87 at hour 2, 0.73–0.93 through the UTC morning, and 2.0–2.4 in the evening. The p90 grows from 1.28 at hour 2 to tens or hundreds in daytime/evening, which is why quantiles come from the empirical spread instead of a narrow parametric band. GTA VI's pre-release category level and pre-checkpoint channel ratios do not provide a reliable launch-scale estimate in the four practice cases; hour 1 is used directly. The GTA VI pre-release series has a 24-hour median of 1,553 viewers at its last checkpoint, with recent spikes; it does not directly set launch magnitude. In practice, hour 1 was 1.3–1.6 times each launch’s recent 24-hour median, while known channels/viewers ranged from 0.010 to 0.027. The practice cases show a run-up into hour 1, but four observations are too few to justify a fitted trend or channels correction.

Local validation: the four kit cases emitted 23 ordered hours each, valid monotone quantiles, and byte-identical output on two runs (0.11–0.14 seconds per run on the development machine). Empty-history/comparable cases at hour-1 counts 1 and 5,000,000 also emitted valid forecasts. The program uses only Python's standard library and embeds no external calibration files.
